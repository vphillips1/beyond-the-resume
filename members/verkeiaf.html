About Me

My journey into software development began in 2020 when I attended a workshop that introduced me to the world of coding and sparked an interest that would eventually lead to a career change. That experience motivated me to pursue programming more seriously and take the next step toward becoming a software developer.

To strengthen my technical foundation, I enrolled in an intensive six-month C# .NET bootcamp, where I developed skills in C# programming, SQL database management, and the .NET Framework. Following the bootcamp, I transitioned into an enterprise environment, where I spent over two years gaining hands-on experience, working with different technologies, and learning how software development operates in a professional setting.

As a software engineer, I enjoy solving problems, building reliable applications, and exploring ways technology can improve the user experience. I particularly enjoy user interface development and the process of turning ideas into functional, meaningful solutions.

Beyond my technical experience, I value collaboration, adaptability, and continuous learning. I enjoy working with others, sharing ideas, and challenging myself to grow both professionally and personally. My goal is to continue expanding my skills as a developer while contributing to projects that create meaningful and lasting solutions.
